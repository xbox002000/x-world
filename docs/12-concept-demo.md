## Interactive demo flow (fictional, clickable)

1. Land on `/demo` with MockProvider.
2. Tutorial tooltip: drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, click to select.
3. Auto-select highest Opportunity node after 3s if idle.
4. Accept quest, confirm action, see graph delta.
5. CTA: Join waitlist (no OAuth required for concept).

## Success criteria

- Viewer understands NBA in under 20s of watching.
- No dashboard-first framing.
- No implication of auto-growth bots.
- Runs offline with bundled JSON.
